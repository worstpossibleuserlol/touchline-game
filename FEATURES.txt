TOUCHLINE — GAME FEATURES
Version 2.4

==================================================

1. FOOTBALL CLUB MANAGEMENT

- Manage your own football club.
- Monitor club finances.
- Manage squad progression.
- Improve club facilities.
- Develop your club over time.

2. PLAYER MANAGEMENT

- Manage your players.
- Monitor player condition.
- Develop young talents.
- Promote academy prospects.
- Maintain squad readiness.

3. SCOUTING SYSTEM

- Discover potential players.
- Review scouting reports.
- Track shortlisted prospects.
- Build toward future squad improvements.

4. YOUTH ACADEMY

- Develop young players.
- Discover new academy prospects.
- Promote promising talents into the senior squad.

5. MATCHDAY EXPERIENCE

- Play interactive arcade-style football matches.
- Set up your team before matches.
- Choose available match presets.
- Pass, shoot, tackle, sprint, and switch players.
- Build attacks and compete on the pitch.

6. STADIUM MANAGEMENT

- Upgrade stadium facilities.
- Manage stadium capacity.
- Monitor attendance.
- Invest in your club's infrastructure.

7. FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

- Monitor club finances.
- Manage available funds.
- Make decisions about club improvements.
- Balance spending and development.

8. SAVE SYSTEM

- Automatic local browser progress storage.
- Export save backups.
- Import compatible backups.
- Improved handling of incomplete or invalid save data.

9. ACCESSIBILITY AND COMPATIBILITY

- Standalone HTML game.
- Designed for modern web browsers.
- Desktop and mobile-friendly interface.
- No dedicated server required for core gameplay.

10. DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES

Touchline development focuses on:

- Gameplay stability.
- Better user experience.
- More management depth.
- Improved matchday systems.
- Save-data reliability.
- Future feature expansion.
